### **1. Trading Concepts Defined**
* **Liquidity Sweep (01:25 - 01:30):** 
  * *Definition:* A high or a low getting taken out, showing the potential for orders to get filled.
  * *Identification/Chart Description:* A price movement that pierces or sweeps past a previous swing high or swing low.
* **Break of Structure (BOS) (01:35 - 01:45):** 
  * *Definition:* Confirmation that orders were filled, accompanied by a market structure shift.
  * *Identification/Chart Description:* Price aggressively breaking structure in the opposite direction of the sweep (e.g., sweeping a high and breaking structure to the downside).
* **Order Block (02:00 - 02:15):** 
  * *Definition:* The price range where orders were filled during the move that caused the liquidity sweep.
  * *Identification/Chart Description:* The specific candle/move up or down that preceded the sweep and subsequent reversal.
* **Fair Value Gap (FVG) (02:15 - 02:40):** 
  * *Definition:* A price range with a lack of liquidity (no counteracting orders in the opposite direction).
  * *Identification/Chart Description:* A large imbalance candle where price left a gap with no overlapping wicks or counteracting orders.
* **Equilibrium (02:40 - 02:50):** 
  * *Definition:* Finding the discounted price between highs and lows.
  * *Identification/Chart Description:* The 50% midpoint level drawn using a fib/retracement tool between a defined high and low.
