### 1. Trading Concepts Defined
* **High:** Formed by a move up followed by a move down (at least 2 candles). Identified by taking the highest wick of whichever of those two candles is higher (01:53, 04:36).
* **Low:** Formed by a move down followed by a move up (at least 2 candles). Identified by taking the lowest wick between the two candles (02:00, 04:50).
* **Break of Structure (BOS):** A shift in the market trend (01:21, 06:58). 
  * **To the downside (uptrend into a downtrend):** Occurs when a single candle body **closes fully below** the most recent low (08:00, 16:00, 18:00). Wicks below lows do not count as a BOS (12:43, 23:30).
  * **To the upside (downtrend into an uptrend):** Occurs when a single candle body **closes fully above** the most recent high (08:15, 17:38). Wicks above highs do not count as a BOS (12:31).
