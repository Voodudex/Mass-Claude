### 1. Trading Concepts Defined
*   **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance:**
    *   *Definition:* An area on the chart where price moved so rapidly in one direction that orders were left unfilled, creating an imbalance. 
    *   *How it is identified/drawn:* Look for a three-candle sequence where the wicks of the first and third candles do not overlap, leaving a gap. Overlapping wicks mean no FVG exists (02:10).
    *   *Usage:* Primarily used as a *retracement tool* and confirmation rather than a direct blind entry (05:20, 15:50).
*   **Break of Structure (BOS):**
    *   *Definition:* When price breaks a previous swing high or swing low, indicating a continuation or shift in trend.
    *   *How it is identified/drawn:* A candle close beyond a significant high or low structure point on a given timeframe.
