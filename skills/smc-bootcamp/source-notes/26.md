* **Equilibrium:** 
  * **Definition (as taught):** A retracement tool used to measure the 50% midpoint of a swing high to swing low (or swing low to swing high) to find premium and discount price zones where big money/market movers are likely to re-enter/buy or sell [05:40–07:50].
