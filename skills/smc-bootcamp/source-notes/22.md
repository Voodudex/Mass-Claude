### **1. Trading Concepts Defined**

*   **Order Block:**
    *   **Definition (as taught):** The move prior to the liquidity sweep (or the move that causes the liquidity sweep) and prior to the break of structure/market structure shift that changes direction. This price range is where institutional orders were able to get filled to push price in the desired direction, and where price will likely retrace to fill more orders [01:30–02:50, 22:15–22:40].
    *   **How it is identified/drawn on the chart:** Look for a liquidity sweep (taking out previous highs or lows) followed by a strong break of structure (BOS) in the opposite direction. The order block is the entire opposing leg/downward candlestick (in an uptrend play) or upward candlestick (in a downtrend play) that preceded the break of structure [04:10–05:00, 07:10–07:40].
    *   *Scaling down:* Higher time-frame order blocks (e.g., 4-hour or daily) provide broad price ranges. Traders can scale down to lower time frames (like the 1-hour or 15-minute) to find more precise internal structure breaks and entries [11:10–12:50].
