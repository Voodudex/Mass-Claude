### **1. Trading Concepts Defined**
* **Break of Structure (BOS):** 
  * *Definition:* When price pushes past and closes beyond a previous significant swing high or low, signaling continuation of the trend or a market shift.
  * *Chart Identification:* Look for a candlestick body to break and close past a previous structural level on the chart.
* **Higher/Lower Timeframe Confluence (Contradictory Structure):**
  * *Definition:* When a lower timeframe (e.g., 15-minute) breaks structure in the opposite direction of a higher timeframe (e.g., hourly), it often acts merely as a **retrace** for the higher timeframe rather than a full trend reversal (04:40 – 06:00).
* **Equilibrium:** 
  * *Definition:* The 50% midpoint of a dealing range or premium/discount zone, used as a potential reaction area for retracements (04:10, 07:05).
* **Imbalance / Fair Value Gap (FVG):**
  * *Definition:* An area of price inefficiency where price rapidly moved through, leaving single-sided price action that price tends to return to/fill (01:30, 07:00).
* **Asian & London Session Highs/Lows:**
  * *Definition:* Key liquidity pools formed during specific trading sessions (07:45 – 08:10).
* **Order Block:**
  * *Definition:* Institutional candle origin before a strong impulsive move where price can react upon a retest (01:05, 08:35).
