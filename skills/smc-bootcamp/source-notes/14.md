* **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Liquidity Void / Imbalance:** These terms are used interchangeably (02:00–02:10). 
  * **Definition:** A price range or void where there are a lack of opposing/contradicting orders during a strong impulsive move (e.g., in a massive upward move, there are no resting sell orders; in a massive downward move, there are no resting buy orders) (02:20–03:00).
  * **Chart Identification:** It is typically identified as a large, impulsive candle (up or down) with no hesitation or overlapping opposing orders (02:35–02:55, 05:32–05:50). Price later retraces back into this range (05:50–05:58).
  * **Purpose:** Used primarily to understand where price wants to draw back during retracements and to locate areas where market makers can fill orders to push price in the trend's direction (03:10–03:38, 04:00–04:08).
