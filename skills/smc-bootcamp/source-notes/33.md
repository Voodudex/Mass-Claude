*   **Liquidity Sweep:**

*   **Break of Structure (BOS):** 
    *   *Definition:* A strong structural break confirming a change or continuation in trend direction.
    *   *Chart Identification:* Price closing past a significant recent swing high/low. (e.g., 07:05, 08:15, 09:56)
*   **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance:** 
    *   *Definition:* An inefficiency in price action where price moved rapidly, leaving a gap between candles.
    *   *Chart Identification:* The space between the wick of candle one and the wick of candle three in a three-candle sequence. (e.g., 07:07, 08:19, 12:47)
*   **Order Block (OB):** 
    *   *Definition:* The last opposing candle (or cluster) before a strong impulsive move that breaks structure.
    *   *Chart Identification:* The highlighted block/candle preceding an impulsive displacement. (e.g., 12:05, 13:21, 22:30)
*   **Equilibrium:** 
    *   *Definition:* The 50% midpoint of a specific dealing range (swing high to swing low).
